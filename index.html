VALORANT Companion for Overwolf

SpikeCoach

Understand your matches. Improve every round.

SpikeCoach is an independent VALORANT companion for stats, learning, and post-match review. During a match, see live statistics, Personal Stats, and scoreboard information through Overwolf. When the match is over, get personalized recommendations, use planning tools, Guess the Rank, and talk to the AI Coach (not during an active match).

Built as an Overwolf Native companion application. Match data comes from information exposed to SpikeCoach through Overwolf, not from Riot account linking. Riot account connection is planned for a future version and is Coming Soon; it is not available today.

Features

Stats and info while you play. Review and learning tools when the match is done. No in-round tactical commands.

  • Live Match Statistics

    See live match stats, Personal Stats, and scoreboard information in the SpikeCoach Overwolf overlay. Informational data only: no rotation, buy, or ability advice during the round.

  • Post-Match Recommendations

    Personalized recommendations are generated only after the match ends. SpikeCoach reviews your performance and highlights patterns and areas to improve for future matches, not for in-round decisions.

  • Impact Rating

    SpikeCoach's custom Impact Rating is a match-impact metric from available performance statistics. It is a SpikeCoach metric only, not MMR, Elo, skill rating, or any official Riot Games or VALORANT rating.

  • AI Coach

    AI Coach is unavailable while a VALORANT match is in progress. Outside an active match, ask general VALORANT questions, talk strategy, or reflect on past games. It is for learning, not live tactical coaching.

  • Strategy Planner

    Place agents and utility on maps to plan setups and strategies before you queue.

  • Lineups

    Browse lineups for common agents, abilities, maps, and sites.

  • Guess the Rank

    Watch gameplay clips and guess the player's rank.

Built for Fair Play

SpikeCoach keeps live gameplay focused on information, not instructions. AI Coach is unavailable while a VALORANT match is in progress, and personalized recommendations are generated only after the match ends. Live match features are limited to displaying allowed statistics and match information.

How It Works

Launch through Overwolf, play VALORANT, then review when the match is over.

  1. 1

    Launch SpikeCoach

    Open SpikeCoach through Overwolf when you are ready to play.

  2. 2

    Play VALORANT

    During the match, SpikeCoach shows live statistics, Personal Stats, and scoreboard information from Overwolf. AI Coach stays off until the match ends.

  3. 3

    Review Your Match

    After the match, review stats and personalized recommendations generated post-match to see what to work on next.

About SpikeCoach

SpikeCoach helps VALORANT players understand what happened in a match and what to improve next time. Live overlays show permitted stats during play. After the match, recommendations and review tools help you reflect and plan. SpikeCoach does not play for you or tell you what to do in the middle of a round.
